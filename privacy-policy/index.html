Privacy Policy

How we use contact information for youth and parent communications.

Who we are

This privacy policy describes how the combined youth programs of Mission Lake Ward and Foothill Ranch Ward (the “Wards,” “we,” or “us”) collect and use contact information to communicate with Young Men, Young Women, leaders, and parents about ward youth activities and logistics.

Messaging tools are operated by ward leaders and advisors using Golden Lantern / Lead Connector and TextLink SMS on behalf of the Wards. Contact for privacy questions: jeremy@miner.tv.

Information we collect

We may store and use:

  • Names of youth, parents/guardians, and leaders
  • Mobile phone numbers and email addresses
  • Program tags or group membership (for example, Young Men, Young Women, parents, quorum/class, ward)
  • Message delivery history needed to run communications (sent/received SMS or email)

We do not knowingly sell personal information. We do not use this contact list for commercial advertising unrelated to ward youth programs.

Where information comes from

Contact details may come from:

  • The Church directory as visible to authorized ward leaders
  • Information parents or youth provide to advisors
  • Existing youth program lists maintained by the Wards

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Send SMS and email about Mutual, activities, service projects, assignments, and logistics
  • Reach parents/guardians when appropriate for safety and coordination
  • Maintain accurate group lists so the right people receive the right messages

Text messaging (SMS)

By providing a mobile number to ward youth leaders or remaining on a ward youth or parent contact list used for program communications, you consent to receive SMS messages from Mission Lake Ward and Foothill Ranch Ward youth programs about activities and logistics. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

  • Opt out: Reply STOP to cancel SMS. You may receive a one-time confirmation.
  • Help: Reply HELP for help, or email jeremy@miner.tv.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Email

We may send program emails to addresses associated with youth or parents. You can request removal from email lists by contacting jeremy@miner.tv.

Sharing

We use service providers (including Golden Lantern / Lead Connector and TextLink SMS) solely to store contacts and deliver messages. We do not sell, rent, or share contact lists with third parties for their marketing.

Retention

We keep contact information while a person is part of the youth program or a parent/guardian contact, and for a reasonable period afterward for continuity. You may request correction or deletion by emailing jeremy@miner.tv.

Children and youth

This program serves youth and their parents/guardians. We communicate about church youth activities. Parents/guardians may request that a youth’s number or email be removed or that messages go only to a parent contact.

Changes

We may update this policy as tools or practices change. The “Last updated” date below will change when we do. The current version will be posted at this URL.
